Privacy Policy

Information about using the SourceTX website and how submitted information is handled.

Effective date: July 19, 2026

SourceTX collects information you voluntarily provide through job applications, résumé submissions, hiring requests, and contact forms. This may include your name, contact information, employment history, résumé, work authorization response, and message content.

How we use information

We use submitted information to evaluate employment opportunities, communicate with candidates and clients, provide recruiting and staffing services, operate the website, maintain records, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing

Candidate information may be shared with prospective client organizations only in connection with relevant employment opportunities and, where appropriate, after discussion with the candidate. We may also use service providers that support hosting, email, security, and business operations.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for recruiting, business, legal, and compliance purposes. We use administrative and technical safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by emailing info@sourcetx.com. Some records may be retained where required by law or legitimate business needs.

International users

Information may be processed in countries other than where it was submitted. By using the website, you acknowledge that applicable privacy protections may differ by jurisdiction.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to info@sourcetx.com.
